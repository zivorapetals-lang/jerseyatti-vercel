Questions

How do I place an order?

Send us a DM on Instagram with the jersey, size and quantity. We’ll confirm the details with you there.

Do you ship to my city?

Yes, we ship pan India.

Can you make jerseys for my team?

Yes, team jersey orders are undertaken. Send your team name, crest, colours, player names, numbers and sizes.

What is the difference between authentic and retro?

Authentic jerseys are modern club and national kits. Retro jerseys are classic shirts from earlier seasons.

Can I add my own name and number?

Yes. Use the customiser on this page, then send us the message it writes on Instagram.

How do I pick the right size?

Choose from XS to XXL in the customiser. If you are not sure, tell us in the DM and we will help you choose.

How do I look after my jersey?

Wash it inside out in cold water and let it air dry. Keep printed names and numbers away from direct heat.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on the jersey and the quantity. Message us and we’ll share it.
